# CallGuard — Play Store listing

**App name:** CallGuard

**Short description (70 of 80 characters):**
Know who's calling. Silence strangers and spam. No server, no account.

**Full description:**

CallGuard is a phone and SMS app for India. It answers one question first: who is calling?

WHO IS CALLING
- Shows the caller's name from the network (CNAP) when your carrier sends it, plus names from your contacts and from what the app has learned on this phone.
- Labels every name with where it came from: a contact, the network, or the phone.
- A "Who is this?" lookup on the keypad for any number.

BLOCK STRANGERS, IF YOU WANT
- Contacts only: calls and texts from numbers not in your contacts are held back. It is on by default once Contacts access is granted.
- Silence mode: unknown callers ring silently.
- Block and allow lists that you control.

SPAM, SCORED ON YOUR PHONE
- Each incoming call and text gets a spam score computed on the phone. It uses a bundled list of known spam numbers and simple patterns: how often a number calls in a short time, and the hour of the call.
- Likely spam is marked on the incoming-call screen. Spam calls are silenced by default. A setting blocks them outright.
- Scores are estimates and can be wrong.

ONE APP FOR CALLS AND TEXTS
- Default phone app: incoming and in-call screens, a keypad, contacts with an alphabet scroller, and recent calls merged into one list.
- Default messaging app: conversations, compose, and reply from the notification.
- Ignore mode sends texts from non-contacts to a Quiet Inbox, so they stay out of your main inbox until you open it.

WHATSAPP CALLER CARD (OPTIONAL)
- With notification access turned on, CallGuard can show a caller card for WhatsApp calls, using the name from the call notification. You can turn it off in settings.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No server, no account, no sign-in.
- The app does not request the internet permission. Your contacts, call history and messages stay on your phone.
- Lookup from Google Contacts is off by default and can be turned on in settings.

SETUP
CallGuard asks you to make it your default apps. Android shows these choices:
- Default phone app: for calls and the in-call screen.
- Default SMS app: for texts.
- Call screening: checks calls from numbers that are not in your contacts before they ring.

Contacts access lets CallGuard use your contacts for names and for contacts-only mode. Notification access is needed only for the WhatsApp caller card. The overlay permission, also optional, shows that card over other apps.

LIMITS
- Caller names depend on the network sending them. CallGuard cannot identify every number. There is no online lookup database.
- Spam scores are estimates on your device, not a guarantee.
- Requires Android 10 or newer.
